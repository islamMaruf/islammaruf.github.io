I'm a Senior Software Engineer based in Dhaka, Bangladesh, currently working at Miaki Media Ltd as an augmented resource for Grameenphone, where I help build and scale the MyGP Payment Orchestration Layer — integrating 10+ payment gateways and processing millions of transactions for over 50 million subscribers.
